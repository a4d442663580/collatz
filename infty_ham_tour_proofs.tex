\section*{Cotree density test}

Definitions, all in terms of $c$ (where $c \ge 4$):
\begin{align*}
  a &= c - 1, \\
  v &= \log_2(c) - 1, \\
  A^- &= a^2 - a - 1 = (c-1)^2 - (c-1) - 1, \\
  \text{lcm}_{LU} &= v \cdot \frac{c^3}{2} \cdot (c-1)^2, \\
  \text{lcm}_{pq} &= \frac{c}{2}\,(c-1)^2.
\end{align*}
